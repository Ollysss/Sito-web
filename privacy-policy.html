At COL Games, we believe privacy is a fundamental right. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard the data of our clients and website visitors. This policy applies to all services provided by COL Games, including our consulting engagements, software development lifecycle (SDLC) management, and managed cloud environments.

3. GDPR & Data Protection Rights

Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located within the European Economic Area (EEA), you have specific rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access

Request copies of your personal data held in our secure infrastructure.

Right to Erasure

Request the permanent deletion of your data under specific conditions.

Data Portability

Request the transfer of your data to another organization or directly to you.

Non-Disclosure

Opt-out of any processing activities not essential to service delivery.

We implement advanced encryption-at-rest (AES-256) and encryption-in-transit (TLS 1.3) for all client data environments. Access is strictly controlled via Zero Trust architecture and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
